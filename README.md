## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Problem:** I can't connect my glasses to the app.
**Solution:** Make sure your glasses are charged and in pairing mode (hold the button for 5 seconds). Restart both the glasses and the app, then retry.

**Problem:** My stream lags or buffers.
**Solution:** Lower the stream quality in the app's Settings (e.g., from 1080p to 720p). Check your internet speed with a quick online test—you need at least 5 Mbps upload.

**Problem:** No audio or video from the glasses.
**Solution:** In the app, go to Settings > Source and confirm "Glasses" is selected. On your phone, check that microphone and camera permissions are granted to the companion app.

**Problem:** The app won't open on my PC.
**Solution:** Right-click the app file and select "Run as administrator." If that fails, download the file again to ensure it's not corrupted.

## 🛠️ Technical Details for Curious Users

Meta-stream is built with Swift and SwiftUI on iOS, and uses HaishinKit for low-latency H.264/HEVC encoding. It supports hardware acceleration on modern devices, meaning your battery lasts longer and your stream stays smooth. The RTMP protocol ensures compatibility with virtually every streaming service, from big platforms to custom servers. The app automatically detects your glasses' video feed and adjusts bitrate in real-time to match your network conditions.
