Build your own.

Four steps. No Mac at any point.

  1. Fork the repoPick a bundle ID. Everything else is a secret you add to the fork.
  2. Register with MetaFree Wearables Developer Center project. Turn on Developer Mode in the Meta AI app.
  3. Sign with a paid Apple teamYours or a friend's. The glasses' Wi-Fi link needs two entitlements only paid teams get.
  4. PushGitHub Actions builds and signs an encrypted IPA. Decrypt it and install over USB.
